Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Hermes Agent calendar recap operated by Rémi Arnould. Contact: remi.arnould2@gmail.com.

Data accessed and purpose

With the account owner's consent, the assistant can read Google Calendar events and Gmail messages and metadata, including subjects and event-related information. It may also read iCloud Calendar events. The data is used to prepare and deliver personal agenda summaries, identify scheduling conflicts and provide relevant event context. Google permissions are read-only; the recap does not send or modify email or modify calendars.

Storage, sharing and security

Authorization credentials are stored on the operator's host with restricted access. Calendar data, generated recaps and operational logs may be stored on that host. Relevant event or message information may be sent to the configured AI service to produce a recap and to Telegram to deliver it. Those providers process data according to their own policies. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Retention and control

Credentials and locally stored data remain until removed or no longer needed for the integration; logs and Telegram messages may persist according to their respective retention settings. The account owner can revoke access through their Google Account's third-party connections and contact the operator to request deletion of locally held integration data. Revoking access stops future Google API reads but does not automatically erase existing messages or logs.

Google user data

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
