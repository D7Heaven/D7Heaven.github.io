2. What Beanboard does

Beanboard is an operations platform for coffee shops and similar food-service businesses. It helps teams manage tasks, checklists, alerts, deliveries, SOPs, training, surveys, onboarding, internal notifications, push messages, and AI-assisted operational guidance.

3. Personal data we collect

Depending on how your organization uses Beanboard, we may collect and process:

  • Account data: name, email address, password hash, role, tenant or store membership, default tenant, account status, and access tokens.
  • Authentication and security data: login events, logout events, request IDs, IP addresses, device or browser information, error logs, and security logs.
  • Operational data: tasks, task completions, time slots, comments, alert records, delivery records, status updates, dates, survey answers, onboarding state, training progress, quiz attempts, and user evaluations.
  • Uploaded content: photos, images, PDFs, files, and other media attached to tasks, alerts, deliveries, SOPs, quiz options, and push messages.
  • Notification data: notification settings, push message recipients, read or unread status, scheduling data, delivery reports, action links, and push tokens or aliases handled through the notification provider.
  • AI feature data: AI chat questions, recent conversation context, generated answers, related SOP references, delivery information used to draft delivery claim emails, image evidence submitted to AI features, model identifiers, provider response IDs, token counts, failure codes, and usage events.
  • Device and app data: mobile platform, app version, timezone sent by the app, local preferences, and diagnostics needed to operate the service.
  • Support and administration data: messages you send to us, support requests, organization configuration, tenant settings, and external links configured by admins.

The current Beanboard code does not show advertising tracking or third-party analytics cookies. The web app uses browser storage for authentication and preferences. The mobile app uses device storage for authentication and local app state.

4. Camera, photos, notifications, and device permissions

The mobile app may request camera or photo permissions so users can capture or upload evidence for tasks, alerts, deliveries, SOPs, and similar operational records. Photos and files you choose to upload are stored and shown to authorized users in your organization.

The mobile app may request notification permission to send work-related push notifications. Push notifications are provided through OneSignal. OneSignal may process device identifiers, push tokens, notification interaction data, technical logs, and similar delivery data.

Beanboard does not currently provide a location-tracking feature in its own backend. If an operating system or notification SDK requests location-related permission, it should be used only for notification infrastructure or platform behavior unless a future Beanboard feature clearly asks for location access.

5. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide tenant-scoped access, roles, permissions, and organization settings.
  • Operate tasks, alerts, delivery workflows, SOPs, training, surveys, onboarding, and reporting.
  • Store and display comments, files, photos, and other content uploaded by users.
  • Send in-app notifications and push notifications based on user and organization settings.
  • Provide AI-assisted features such as SOP questions, AI chat, AI tag generation, quiz generation, alert suggestions, and delivery claim email drafts.
  • Monitor service performance, prevent abuse, debug errors, keep the service secure, and maintain auditability.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about the service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

7. AI features and third-party AI providers

Beanboard uses OpenAI services for AI-assisted features. When a user invokes an AI feature, relevant input may be sent to OpenAI, such as the user question, selected SOP context, recent AI chat context, delivery details, comments, dates, media evidence, or other operational content needed to generate the requested output.

AI outputs can be inaccurate or incomplete. Users and organizations remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before relying on it, sending it, or using it in operational decisions. Do not submit passwords, payment data, government identifiers, health data, or other unnecessary sensitive data into AI prompts or uploaded evidence.
